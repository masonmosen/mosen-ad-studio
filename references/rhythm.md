# Rhythm — act lengths, cut contrast, accents, review

## R1 · Act-length contrast

A film where every act lasts the same feels like a slideshow even when each
frame is beautiful. Across the act list:

- longest act ≥ 1.8 × the median act
- shortest act ≤ 0.45 × the median act
- at least one **burst** (≥ 3 cuts with intervals ≤ 0.6s)
- at least one **long hold** (a single image carried by drift/push ≥ 4s)

Voiceover-driven films take their grid from the read instead — measure the
VO's phrase boundaries (silence detection) and cut on them; declare the
deviation in the shot list rather than forcing the read into a beat grid.

## R4 · Cut contrast

Adjacent acts must differ in at least one of: shot scale (wide ↔ macro),
light/colour (dark ↔ bright stage), motion energy (still ↔ violent), with
an energy delta ≥ 2 levels on a 1–5 scale. Two calm acts in a row is where
viewers leave.

## R5 · Transition quota

- ≥ 2 transition families in the film (e.g. hard-cut family + reveal family)
- ≥ 1 **impact transition** — hard cut + 2–4 frame flash, or a scale punch —
  placed on the signature moment
- the same family ≤ 3 uses; a single elegant wipe across all six acts is the
  P7 failure (poster frames fine, film flat)

## Sync points

Every act boundary and signature transition gets a sync point in the shot
list: time, picture event, sound event. The sound event is M6 (below).

## Review — the 8 dimensions

Score the draft 1–5 per dimension. Pass line: dimensions **1, 4, 6, 8 ≥ 4**,
the rest ≥ 3.

1. Concept legibility (can a stranger repeat the idea?)
2. Poster frames (does each act have a still that survives the IG grid?)
3. Product anchoring (is the product the lead, ≥ 60%?)
4. Rhythm (contrast per R1/R4 — felt, not measured)
5. Sound & motif (would you recognise the film with your eyes closed?)
6. Transitions (variety per R5, impact where it matters)
7. Type & copy (official lines only, set like a poster)
8. **力道 Punch** — do the cuts land? Is there at least one moment you'd
   rewind? A film can pass 1–7 and still be forgettable; 8 is the veto.

**Frame discipline for review:** extract settled frames *and* transition
midpoints *and* act-boundary ±0.2s frames. Reviewing only settled frames is
how a one-wipe film ships. Same-family transitions throughout = veto
regardless of scores.
