# Brief — the concept gate (G1)

No code is written before a brief exists. The brief is one page; if the
concept can't survive one page, it won't survive twenty seconds.

## Required fields

1. **One-line concept** — a sentence a stranger can repeat after one viewing.
   Not a mood ("premium, minimal") — a mechanism ("the world shakes, the
   product doesn't"). If the sentence needs the word "and", there are two
   concepts; pick one.
2. **Product anchoring** — the product's on-screen share (target ≥ 60%), what
   the visual lead is (must be the product itself, in real official imagery),
   and where the signature moment lands (must be on the product).
3. **Signature transition** — one move taken from the product's own shape or
   behaviour (an aperture iris for a camera phone, a glaze sweep for a serum,
   a click cut for a tool with detents). It carries ≥ 3 exits and is named in
   the shot list.
4. **Hook** — pick from `hooks.md` (H1–H10) and say exactly what is on screen
   at 0.0–2.0s. "Logo animation" is not a hook.
5. **Motif** — the sound idea in one line (three notes, a texture, a rhythm
   cell), with its three appearances scheduled (teaser ≤ 3s / full form at
   the signature moment / resolve at the lockup).
6. **Colour discipline** — stage colour (sampled from the product's own
   imagery), one accent, and what is *banned* (usually: rainbow palettes,
   stock gradients, the previous film's stage).

## The product gate （选品关） — check before the brief

| gate | pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| On sale | buyable now, mainstream channel, current model |
| Recognisable | the audience knows the brand/product without a caption |
| Official assets | ≥ 6 angles of official imagery + ≥ 1 official video or lifestyle asset |
| Resolution | stills ≥ 1600px on the long edge, or video ≥ 1080p |
| Identity | official palette and typeface are knowable |

A product that fails the asset gate will produce a concept film, not an ad —
this is the P6 failure (a code-drawn dot-matrix phone that nobody can buy).
When in doubt, pick the product with the richer official asset library over
the more interesting product.

## Approval

The client confirms the brief in one sentence (G1). If nobody confirms, the
strongest concept proceeds and the film is delivered marked
"concept unconfirmed". A rejected concept returns here — never patch a dead
concept downstream with more effects.
