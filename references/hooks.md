# Hook library H1–H10 — the first two seconds

Pick one in the brief; say exactly what is on screen at 0.0–2.0s. Proven
patterns from the first nine Studio productions and the reference teardowns
behind them.

| # | Hook | Shape | Production example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Macro texture first | an extreme close-up of the product's material fills frame 1, pull back to reveal | La Mer swirl macro |
| H2 | Human + product, mid-action | a person already *using* the product — no wind-up | Rhode application footage |
| H3 | Problem in one frame | the pain, visualised (a shaking world, a filthy keyboard) | Pocket 4 judder open |
| H4 | The lead looks at you | a face, direct or profile, with a headline that speaks for them | MERIDIAN desk portrait |
| H5 | Scale contrast | the product impossibly big or small against its world | Nothing's 489-dot count |
| H6 | Number slam | one hard number, full screen, counted or smashed in | "4 Tools." / 240fps |
| H7 | Before/after in one cut | the transformation as a single hard cut, no dissolve | glaze sweep reveals |
| H8 | Motion in frame 1 | the product already moving (spin, pour, slide) — stills are for posters | Olipop turntable |
| H9 | Texture + word | one tactile close-up + one loaded word | "The Glaze" |
| H10 | Sound first | the motif's teaser *is* the hook; picture follows the sound | click-cell openings |

## Anti-hooks (banned)

- Logo sting first. Nobody owes a logo their first two seconds.
- Fade from black. It reads as "the video hasn't started".
- A wide empty stage with small centred product. The grid will eat it.
- "Coming soon" energy — teasing a product the film is supposed to sell.
